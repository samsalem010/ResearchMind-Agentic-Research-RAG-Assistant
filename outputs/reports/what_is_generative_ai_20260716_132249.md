**Executive Summary**

Generative AI, often referred to as GenAI, is a type of artificial intelligence that creates original content, such as text, images, video, audio, or software code, in response to a user's prompt or request. **Background/Overview**
=====================

Generative AI works by using deep learning models, specifically neural networks, to learn patterns, structures, and relationships from vast datasets, then predict and generate new content that mirrors those learned patterns in response to user prompts ([1](#), [2](#)). This probabilistic sequence-prediction system breaks down input into smaller units called tokens, calculates the probability of different features occurring together, and learns the underlying distribution of data features to generate coherent outputs ([2](#), [6](#)). **Detailed Analysis**
==================

Generative AI relies on deep learning models and neural networks that simulate human brain processes to identify and encode patterns in huge amounts of data ([1](#), [2](#)). The models analyze training data to understand relationships and structures, then predict what word, sound, or pixel should come next in a pattern to create novel content ([5](#), [7](#)). The system draws from this encoded knowledge to produce outputs that are statistically probable and often indistinguishable from human-created content ([2](#), [10](#)).
