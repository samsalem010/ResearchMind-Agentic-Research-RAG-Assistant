**Best Source:** [Amdahl's Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amdahl%27s_law)
**Summary:** Amdahl's Law provides a theoretical upper bound on the maximum speedup that can be achieved by parallel processing, assuming a fixed problem size and ignoring parallelization overhead. It states that the maximum speedup is limited by the fraction of the program that cannot be parallelized, and is often used to identify performance bottlenecks and guide code optimization strategies. The law is a fundamental principle in parallel computing and is used to evaluate the potential benefits of parallel processing.
