**Best Source:** [Amdahl's Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amdahl%27s_law)
**Summary:** Amdahl's Law is a theoretical bound on the maximum speedup that can be achieved by parallel processing, assuming a fixed problem size and zero overhead for parallelization. It provides a formula to calculate the maximum theoretical speedup, taking into account the fraction of the program that cannot be parallelized. The law is often used to identify performance bottlenecks and guide code optimization strategies in parallel computing.
